# CostPilot Dreamforce Wow Demo Script

## Purpose

This script is for a quick hallway, coffee-table, or phone demo at Dreamforce. The goal is not to show every feature. The goal is to make one idea obvious:

CostPilot helps companies scale AI agents without losing control of cost, risk, and accountability.

## Target Demo Length

3 minutes.

If the person is interested, continue into the dashboard, reports, trial flow, or pilot conversation.

## The Core Story

A company has AI agents working across sales and support. A messy customer request comes in. Without CostPilot, the company may not know what model handled it, whether sensitive data was present, whether the request could have been cheaper, or whether anyone can audit the decision later.

CostPilot evaluates the request, routes it, prunes waste, detects risk, and records the decision.

For the live Dreamforce demo, use two short stories instead of forcing one payload to prove everything:

- Money saved story: routine support request routes to Scout instead of a premium tier.
- Governance story: messy renewal request triggers pruning, risk detection, escalation, and audit proof.

## Opening Line

"The problem I am focused on is what happens after companies deploy AI agents everywhere. At some point, leaders need to know who used AI, what it cost, whether the request was risky, and whether it could have been handled more efficiently."

## Step 1: Show The Routine Money-Saved Request

Screen to show:

- Live demo or fake CRM page.
- The routine account access status update scenario.

What to say:

"First, here is a simple support request. The customer only needs a short status update. This is the kind of routine AI work that can happen thousands of times across a company."

What the viewer should notice:

- This feels like real CRM work.
- It is not an abstract AI demo.
- Simple work should not require a premium model.

## Step 2: Run The Routine Request Through CostPilot

Screen to show:

- Submit or run action.
- CostPilot decision result.

What to say:

"Instead of sending this directly to the most expensive model, CostPilot evaluates the request first. In this case, it recognizes the work as routine and routes it to Scout."

What the viewer should notice:

- CostPilot is acting before the AI call.
- There is a decision point.
- The request is governed, not invisible.

## Step 3: Show The Routing Savings Decision

Screen to show:

- Tier selected: Scout, Analyst, Advisor, or Strategist.
- Risk level.
- Outcome.
- Rationale.

What to say:

"This is the first savings story: not every AI request deserves the same model or the same cost. Routine work can stay efficient."

What the viewer should notice:

- Routing connects directly to cost control.
- Higher-risk work still gets handled carefully.
- The rationale explains why the decision happened.

## Step 4: Show The Governance Request

Screen to show:

- The renewal request story.
- Messy email thread.
- Contract or legal language.

What to say:

"Now here is a different kind of request. It is still a normal business workflow, but this one has messy email history and risk language. This is where governance matters."

What the viewer should notice:

- CostPilot does more than route cheap tasks.
- It can also detect when work needs more care.

## Step 5: Show Pruning

Screen to show:

- Raw tokens.
- Clean tokens.
- Tokens saved.
- Reduction percentage, if visible.

What to say:

"This is one of the easiest places to see waste. A lot of business prompts include repeated headers, old threads, signatures, disclaimers, and empty space. CostPilot strips unnecessary context before the AI call, so the company is not paying for junk."

What the viewer should notice:

- Savings are not theoretical only.
- The product can show before and after context.
- Pruning is simple to understand.

## Step 6: Show Risk or Blocked Handling

Screen to show:

- Sensitive term or blocked request example.
- Rationale explaining why it was blocked or escalated.

What to say:

"If the request includes sensitive information, CostPilot can block it before it reaches an AI model. That matters because companies need more than speed. They need rules of engagement."

What the viewer should notice:

- CostPilot is not just a cost tool.
- It also supports governance and risk control.
- This connects well to executive, legal, and security concerns.

## Step 7: Show Dashboard Proof

Screen to show:

- Executive summary dashboard.
- Requests governed.
- AI spend avoided.
- Routing efficiency.
- Context pruning saved.
- Audit events.

What to say:

"The individual decision is useful, but the bigger value is the roll-up. Leaders can see how many requests were governed, where savings are coming from, what was blocked, and which teams or agents are driving usage."

What the viewer should notice:

- CostPilot turns AI activity into business reporting.
- It gives CFO, CTO, security, and operations teams a shared view.
- The product is more than a demo button.

## 30-Second Close

"The simple version is this: AI agents are going to spread across the business. CostPilot helps companies control the cost, risk, and accountability of that usage before it becomes messy. I am looking for feedback from people who are adopting AI and need visibility into what is really happening."

## Best Demo Scenario

Use a customer renewal or support request with:

- Long email thread.
- Repeated headers.
- Contract or legal language.
- Enough routine information to show pruning.
- Enough risk language to show escalation or governance.

This scenario lets CostPilot show three things in one run:

- Waste reduction.
- Smart routing.
- Auditability.

## What Not To Show First

Avoid starting with:

- Model pricing tables.
- Admin setup.
- Technical architecture.
- Raw database details.
- Too many dashboards at once.

Those can come later if the person asks.

## Best Questions To Ask After The Demo

- "Does this look like a problem your company is likely to face?"
- "Who would own this inside your organization: finance, IT, security, operations, or the AI team?"
- "What proof would you need before this felt worth piloting?"
- "Would you care more about savings, risk controls, or audit visibility?"
- "Where would this fit in your AI adoption process?"

## If They Are A Buyer

Ask:

"If your company deployed AI agents across departments, who would be responsible for explaining the bill and the risk?"

## If They Are An Investor Or Advisor

Ask:

"Do you see this as a product, a feature, or a category that becomes more important as AI usage grows?"

## If They Are In The Salesforce Ecosystem

Ask:

"As Agentforce adoption grows, do you think customers will need more visibility into cost, routing decisions, risk, and audit history?"

## The Memory Hook

"CostPilot is like a flight recorder and cost-control tower for AI agents."

Use this only as a casual line. The cleaner business phrase is:

"CostPilot is a governance and cost-control layer for AI agents."
