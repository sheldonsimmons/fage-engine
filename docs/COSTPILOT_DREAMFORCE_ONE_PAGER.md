# CostPilot One-Pager

## One-Sentence Positioning

CostPilot helps companies control AI agent spend, risk, and accountability by routing requests to the right model, pruning waste, blocking sensitive data, and showing executives what happened.

## The Problem

Companies are starting to deploy AI across support, sales, operations, finance, and internal workflows. As usage grows, leaders need answers to simple questions:

- Who is using AI?
- Which teams and agents are driving cost?
- Are simple tasks being sent to expensive models?
- Is sensitive data being sent into AI tools?
- Can finance, security, and leadership see what happened?

Without visibility, AI adoption can become expensive, risky, and hard to explain.

## What CostPilot Does

CostPilot acts like a control layer for AI requests. Before an AI request is routed, CostPilot evaluates the payload and records the decision.

It helps teams:

- Route routine work to lower-cost model tiers.
- Escalate complex or high-risk work to stronger model tiers.
- Prune messy payloads so unnecessary text does not increase cost.
- Block sensitive data before it reaches an AI model.
- Track each AI decision in an audit log.
- Show executives savings, usage, risk, and governance activity.

## Why It Matters

AI is moving from experiments to daily business operations. That creates a new management problem: companies do not just need AI capability, they need AI control.

CostPilot gives leaders a way to see whether AI is being used responsibly, whether money is being wasted, and whether risky requests are being handled correctly.

## Example Use Case

A sales or support agent receives a long customer renewal request. The message includes repeated email headers, old thread history, signatures, and legal language.

CostPilot can:

1. Strip unnecessary text before the AI request is processed.
2. Detect business risk such as contract, legal, or confidential language.
3. Route the request to the appropriate model tier.
4. Record why that decision was made.
5. Show the business impact in a dashboard.

The result is a clearer AI workflow: less waste, better routing, stronger governance, and a record leadership can review.

## Who It Helps

CostPilot is designed for teams adopting AI agents or AI workflows across business systems.

Primary audiences:

- CTOs and CIOs who need visibility and control over AI usage.
- CFOs who need to understand and manage AI spend.
- Security and compliance teams that need auditability and policy enforcement.
- Operations leaders managing AI agents across departments.
- Salesforce and Agentforce teams looking for governance around AI adoption.

## Current Stage

CostPilot is in MVP/private pilot stage. The product includes a live app, trial flow, onboarding, multi-platform setup, executive dashboard, operational dashboard, audit log, AgentLake registry, reports, demo environment, and upgrade flow.

The current goal is to validate the strongest buyer use cases, prove the cost-savings story clearly, and identify early pilot customers or advisors.
