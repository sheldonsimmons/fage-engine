# CostPilot Podcast Prompt — "The Bill Nobody Can Explain"

Using the document provided, generate a single podcast episode script in the style of a conversational two-host business podcast — similar to How I Built This or Acquired, but shorter, and closer in spirit to an investment case than a company origin story. The audience is CEOs, CFOs, and board members deciding whether AI governance is worth investing in this year — not developers, and not internal team members. This is a sell, not a behind-the-scenes engineering account: do not narrate how the product was built, do not tell "we found a bug and fixed it" stories, and never mention specific model names, API details, or internal architecture.

The episode should:

— **Open** with the cold open in the source document: a CEO asks a simple question in a leadership meeting — what are we actually spending on AI, and is it working — and nobody in the room can answer it. Make this moment land as something the listener has probably already lived through or is about to.

— **State the business problem plainly**: AI is being wired into every system a company runs faster than any company is building a way to govern it. Nobody owns the number, nobody's watching the door for sensitive data, nobody's controlling the pace of spend, and nobody has one unified picture across platforms.

— **Walk through all four use cases from the source document as short, concrete scenarios, not a feature list**: cost attribution (who actually spent this), governance (what almost went out the door), budget control (who's about to blow the budget), and cross-platform unification (why do I have five different stories). Each should feel like a specific moment a listener recognizes, not a product spec.

— **Make the money case** in terms a CFO trusts — not invented precise numbers, but the *shape* of the cost: unmanaged spend is lumpy and back-loaded, the real cost includes the time spent reconstructing what happened after the fact, and a governance layer pays for itself by preventing the overspend and eliminating the archaeology.

— **Look ahead, credibly** — cover the near-term direction from the source document (predictive budget alerts, deeper cross-platform coverage, sharper automatic risk detection, more self-serve financial control) framed as direction the category is heading, not a guaranteed roadmap or a sweeping future vision.

— **Include one honest tension** — governance adds a step to how teams work day to day; the trade is real friction in exchange for real trust and control. Don't let the episode read as pure marketing copy.

— **Close** on the source document's closing question: if unmanaged cloud spend and SaaS sprawl both eventually forced companies to build real governance around them, what does a company risk by waiting to do the same for AI?

Tone: confident, conversational, informal enough to feel like two smart people talking through a real investment decision — not a sales deck read aloud. Grounded in real financial and operational stakes, not hype. Avoid technical jargon entirely. Length: 15–18 minutes of audio script.
