What happens next
  1. Salesforce will ask you to approve access for CostPilot's components — review and click Install.
  2. Once installation finishes, return to CostPilot and continue setup — you'll connect your org and choose what CostPilot should govern.
  3. Nothing is activated automatically. You choose which Agentforce agents or Flows CostPilot governs, and it starts in Observe mode by default.
