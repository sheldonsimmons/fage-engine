The routing model

CostPilot uses a 70/30 routing split as its baseline assumption:

70% Routine calls — FAQs, lookups, summaries, simple tasks — route to Scout (the cheapest capable model at $0.25/$1.25 per 1M tokens)
30% Complex calls — reasoning, drafting, analysis — stay on your current model at full rate
Pricing

Model rates are taken from published Anthropic and OpenAI pricing pages. Your CSV token counts (or manual inputs) are multiplied against those rates to calculate your actual spend.

💡 This is a conservative estimate. CostPilot learns your actual call patterns from day one — if more than 30% of your calls are routine, your real savings will be higher. The dashboard shows live actuals once you're connected.
