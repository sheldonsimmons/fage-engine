Why does this matter? If Salesforce and ServiceNow both try to update the same customer record at the same time, only one should win. CostPilot detects this conflict automatically — your policy below decides what happens next.
🔒 Lock — Stop and alert a supervisor
Both agents freeze. No data is written. Your team gets an alert and decides which agent should proceed. Best for: billing, legal documents, compliance records — anything where getting it wrong has consequences.
⏳ Queue — Wait your turn, then continue
The second agent waits automatically until the first one finishes, then proceeds on its own. No human needed. Best for: routine support tickets, low-stakes updates, high-volume workflows.
⏭ Skip — Move on and log it
The second agent skips this record and moves to the next one. The conflict is recorded in your audit log but nothing is blocked. Best for: non-critical tasks like analytics, reporting, or notifications.
Not sure? Start with Lock. You can change this anytime from the dashboard.
