# FAGE Podcast Prompt

Using the document provided, generate a podcast episode script in the style of a conversational two-host technology business podcast — similar to How I Built This or Acquired, but shorter and more focused. The audience is business leaders, not developers — CTOs, CFOs, operations leaders, and compliance officers who are curious about AI governance but not deeply technical.

The episode should:

— Open with a relatable hook about the chaos of AI adoption inside large companies — costs nobody can explain, compliance teams in the dark, AI bots stepping on each other

— Introduce the concept of AI governance middleware as a new emerging category, not just a product

— Walk through two or three real-world examples that make the value concrete — use the support team cost savings story, the legal department sensitive term story, and the HR SSN detection story

— Emphasize how fast it connects to existing platforms — the message should be "your team is already using Salesforce, ServiceNow, or HubSpot — you don't change any of that, you just add a layer underneath it in an afternoon"

— Talk about the money story in a way a CFO would understand — not technical savings, but real dollar amounts and what companies are leaving on the table without this

— Touch on compliance and audit briefly — frame it as "when the auditor calls, you have an answer"

— Close with a forward-looking take on where AI governance is heading — why this becomes a standard part of every enterprise AI stack, not an optional add-on

Tone: confident, conversational, slightly evangelistic but grounded in real numbers. Avoid technical jargon. Never mention specific model names, API details, or internal architecture. Focus on outcomes, not implementation. Length: 20–22 minutes of audio script.
